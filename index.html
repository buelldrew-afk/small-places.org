About the conference

A conference to encourage those who minister in small places.

Cool is a very small place, about 2,500 people in the Sierra foothills. Most churches in America look a lot more like ours than like the ones you hear about at the big conferences.

It would seem disingenuous to host a conference on small places and then bring in a big name to wow everyone. So we ask pastors and ministry wives who serve in small places to open God’s Word and encourage one another.

One day, on a Wednesday

Many pastors set Saturday aside for family. We won’t ask you to give it up.

A men’s track and a women’s track

Two plenary sessions for everyone, and three breakout seminars in each track.

Small places everywhere

Rural towns, mountain churches, and small congregations in big cities are all welcome.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who can attend?

Anyone. The conference is designed for pastors, ministry leaders, and their wives who serve in small places, and that includes small churches in big cities.

Why a Wednesday?

Many pastors set Saturday aside as a family day. We don’t want to ask you to give that up.

Where should I stay?

Auburn, about 15 minutes from the church, has the closest hotels. The Springhill Suites by Marriott is a good option.

Will it be livestreamed?

Yes. The plenary sessions will stream on the Cool Community Church Facebook page.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes. Audio is posted to the church podcast and website after the conference, and many sessions become articles in the Small-Places Journal.
